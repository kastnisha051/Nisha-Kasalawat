## Implementation & Responsive Guidance
- Page order: Header → Hero → Value Proposition → Services → About & Trust → How It Works → FAQ → Final Consultation CTA → Footer.
- Use one H1, H2s for major sections, and H3s for cards and FAQ questions.
- Keep headlines naturally wrapping; avoid forced desktop line breaks.
- Stack hero text, CTAs, service cards, and process steps on mobile. Keep the primary CTA first.
- Use service grids with 1 column on mobile, 2 on tablet, and up to 3 on desktop.
- Give buttons comfortable tap targets and descriptive accessible names.
- Preserve complete service copy on mobile; avoid hiding key benefits behind hover interactions.
- Repeat the consultation CTA in the header, hero, services closing, process, and final section.
- Build the listed routes before enabling their links. Use #services for the hero’s secondary CTA.
- Connect consultation CTAs to one working booking destination. Do not ship placeholder calendar or form behavior.
- Omit blog previews until real articles are published.
- Do not publish placeholder testimonials, logos, results counters, or unsupported promises.
